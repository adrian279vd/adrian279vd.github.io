About me

I am originally from Cuba and now live in Talca, Chile. I studied mathematics at Universidad de La Habana before moving to Universidad de Talca, where I completed my master's degree and am now pursuing a PhD under the supervision of Jan Felipe van Diejen.

During my undergraduate years, I was especially drawn to applied mathematics, optimization, and programming. Graduate study gradually led me toward more theoretical questions and gave me the opportunity to discover new sides of mathematics.

Outside mathematics, I enjoy football, hiking, video games, and spending time in quiet places.
